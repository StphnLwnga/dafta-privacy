title: Dafta privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Dafta is an Android app that reads your mobile money payment messages and shows them as a list by day and by person. It is made by Vicorp Solutions.

Contact: info@vicorpsolutions.com

## The short version

Everything Dafta reads stays on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data to us or to anyone else.

## What Dafta reads

- Dafta reads payment messages from M-Money, and only from M-Money. It does not read messages from any other sender.
- It reads the messages already on your phone when you first set it up. After that, it reads new M-Money messages as they arrive, also when the app is closed.
- To do this, Android asks you to allow Dafta to read and receive text messages. You can refuse. Without that permission the app cannot list your payments.
- Dafta never sends, deletes or changes any message.
- A one-time code message (OTP) is recognised and skipped. Its text is not stored.

## What Dafta keeps

From each payment message, Dafta keeps the amount, the date and time, whether money came in or went out, the type of payment, the transaction number, any fee, the balance, and the name and phone number written in the message. It also keeps the original message text for payments, so you can read it in the app. If you add a note or a tag to a payment, Dafta keeps that too, and your settings (language, theme, wallet label, and the App lock PIN if you turn it on).

## Where it is kept

Everything is stored in an encrypted database on your phone. The key to the database is kept in the phone's secure storage. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Dafta has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use the internet. It is not included in Android backups, so if you lose your phone, the history in Dafta is lost with it.

The App lock PIN is an extra step to open the app. It keeps out someone who borrows your phone. It is not what encrypts the data.

## What you can send out of the app

You can export your payments as a CSV file, an Excel file or a PDF report. You choose to do this, and you choose where the file goes: to another app through the Android share sheet, or to a folder you pick on your phone. After that, the file is outside Dafta, and it is under the rules of the app or the place you sent it to. We do not receive it.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- Dafta keeps your payments until you delete them.
- In Settings, Delete the app's copy of message texts removes the original message texts from the app. Your payments and totals stay.
- In Settings, Delete everything and start again erases all your payment history and settings from the app, and the key to the database.
- Uninstalling Dafta removes its data from the phone.
- Your own text messages in Android are never changed by any of these.
- Files you have already exported stay where you put them. Delete them there.

## Children

Dafta is a tool for people who handle mobile money payments. It is not made for children, and we do not knowingly collect information about children. Because Dafta sends nothing off the phone, we do not receive any information about anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will change the date at the top and publish the new text at the same address.
